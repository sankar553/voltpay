# Legal Protection Guide for Startup (VoltPay / PowerScan)

Ideas themselves are usually not protected by copyright. What you can protect is your implementation, branding, and inventions.

Here are the best ways to protect your project in India:

## 1. Keep records (Start immediately)

Save:
* Your project documents.
* Design sketches.
* Source code.
* Presentation.
* Dates of creation.

Using GitHub (private repository) or cloud storage with timestamps helps show when you created the work.

## 2. Register a trademark

If you choose a name like VoltPay, you can register it as a trademark. This protects your brand name and logo.

## 3. Copyright

Your:
* Website code
* Mobile app code
* UI designs
* Documents
* PPT

can be protected by copyright.

## 4. Patent (only if applicable)

If your system contains a new and non-obvious technical invention, you may be able to apply for a patent. Simply using QR codes for bill payment is unlikely to be patentable by itself because QR-based payments already exist. However, if you invent a genuinely new technical method, it's worth discussing with a patent professional.

## 5. Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

If you need to show your idea to developers, companies, or investors before launch, consider using an NDA so they agree not to disclose confidential information.

## My advice

Since you're still in the idea stage:

✅ Choose your startup name.
✅ Build a website prototype.
✅ Build a working app prototype.
✅ Prepare a pitch deck.
✅ Then approach the Andhra Pradesh electricity department or investors.

A working prototype is often more valuable than just describing an idea.

I also recommend doing a patentability search before spending money on a patent. It can help determine whether your specific technical approach is likely to qualify.
