About Us

Our Mission & Vision

Building the future of utility payments, one scan at a time.

Making Electricity Bill Payments Simple, Secure & Digital

PowerScan was born from a simple observation: paying electricity bills shouldn't be complicated. In Andhra Pradesh, millions of consumers still struggle with long queues, forgotten consumer numbers, and unreliable payment systems.

We partnered with the Andhra Pradesh Electricity Department to place unique QR codes on every electricity meter. Our platform lets customers scan and pay in seconds — no consumer number required, no hassle, no delays.

Our vision extends beyond Andhra Pradesh. We aim to digitize electricity bill payments across India, making every meter smart and every payment instant.

Vision

To become India's leading QR-based utility payment platform, serving 100 million+ households.

Mission

Eliminate the complexity of bill payments through technology that anyone can use.

Partnership

Official agreement with AP Electricity Department ensuring direct, secure fund transfers.
